# Candidate screen and rationale

Compared: public pollinator-garden stewardship; shared-kitchen food-rescue handoff; community fountain maintenance; public shade-site readiness; shared bicycle parking status. The latter four overlap PantryLink/MarketChill/MealBridge, RefillReady, CoolStop/Heat Relief Network, and CycleGuard. PollinatorPatch centers volunteer-maintained habitat observations rather than beekeeping (HiveSync), street-tree watering (TreeTender), or rain-garden hydrology (RainGarden). It makes no species, biodiversity, or pollinator-health claims; its first use is bed-level conditions plus human-entered bloom context for organizing volunteer visits. Scope excludes automation, imaging, individual tracking, ecological certification, and predictive ML. Soil calibration and field tests remain necessary.
