# Installer and content calibration checklist

1. Choose an installation position with the site accessibility lead; preserve clear accessible paths and tactile/NFC reach range.
2. Check enclosure weather rating and mounting stability; no rating is claimed by this prototype.
3. Verify the external SELV supply, fuse, polarity protection, connector retention and measured audio/radio peak current on the actual assembly.
4. Verify PN532 detection with a test card, button debounce, audio intelligibility at a conservative volume, and physical stop behavior.
5. Have curator review the plain-language text, transcript, language, source/credit, and audio checksum. Content is unavailable until explicitly approved and locally installed.
6. Verify offline playback by disconnecting gateway/network, then verify readiness returns to unknown/stale when telemetry stops.
7. Invite disabled visitors/access consultants to evaluate reach, tactile labels, comprehension, volume and alternatives before public use.

No calibration values, product safety, weatherproofing, radio compliance, accessibility certification, or physical test results are asserted here.
