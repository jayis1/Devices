# Product brief

Problem: volunteer-led recreation programs often share equipment across sessions but lack an easy way to see aggregate stock and items awaiting human inspection. PlayShare is for equipment stewards at parks, schools, and community centers.

User stories:
- As a steward, I can see approximate available stock by rack so I can prepare a session.
- As a volunteer, I can record a manual inspection event so the next steward sees that a person checked the item.
- As a site coordinator, I can see whether the return dock is near capacity and schedule a collection.

Hero metric: reduce time spent on manual pre-session inventory checks; establish a baseline and compare after a site pilot. No improvement claim is made without a controlled evaluation.

Non-goals: borrower tracking, reservations, access control, automated safety approval, identifying damaged gear, payment, or public availability guarantees.

Privacy: no identity, video, audio, or location data. Events are site-local and operator-configured retention applies. Synthetic ML fixtures are not real user data.

Novelty axes: user—equipment steward; job—aggregate sports-gear readiness; interaction—rack scale, return dock, and staff check-in; consequence—availability and inspection workload, not medical, life-safety, or facility control.
