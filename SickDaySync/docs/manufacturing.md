# SickDaySync manufacturing notes

## Enclosures
- Care Hub: ABS desktop enclosure with passive venting and external LTE antenna feedthrough.
- Recovery Band: TPU strap + PC/ABS puck, IP54 splash resistance.
- Room Sentinel: wall-mount wedge enclosure with acoustic port mesh and rear cable exit.
- Med Station: countertop wedge with removable stainless bottle pad and wipe-clean top surface.
- Vent Controller: UL94-V0 DIN/utility enclosure with finger-safe screw terminals.

## DFM considerations
- Keep radar and Sub-GHz antennas isolated from switching regulators.
- Provide conformal-coat keep-out around optical PPG window and acoustic mic ports.
- Use keyed connectors for actuator and relay harnesses.
- Add test pads for SWD and RF conducted test during EVT/DVT.
