# Concept selection

Chosen: transit elevator availability advisory. Multiple fixed lift locations and a separate staff/user landing point require geographically distributed nodes; sensing stays outside lift control systems. Distinct from existing sidewalk-obstruction (CurbCue), public-service queue (QueueKind), and facility readiness (CommonSignal/CivicRest) systems.

Alternatives rejected: community parcel locker status (too close to delivery/security systems); shared laundry availability (WashWise overlap); public water fountain status (ParkPulse overlap); accessible curb condition (CurbCue duplicate); library seat occupancy (LibraryLoop duplicate). This concept is not a safety guarantee and its usefulness requires agency participation.
