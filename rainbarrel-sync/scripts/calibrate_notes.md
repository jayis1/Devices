# Calibration checklist (manual)

1. Record tank internal height and usable capacity from manufacturer documentation.
2. Compare distance sensor readings at several known water heights against a dipstick; characterize dead zone, condensation, and slosh.
3. Confirm rain gauge tip volume with a measured pour, repeat at slow/fast rates, and document uncertainty.
4. Compare demand pulse totals against a measured bucket volume over multiple flow rates.
5. Store calibration date, sensor identity, and uncertainty locally. Recalibrate after sensor replacement or mounting change.

These steps do not establish drinking-water safety or prevent flooding.
