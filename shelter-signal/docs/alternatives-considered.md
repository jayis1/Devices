# Concept selection notes

Selected: transit-shelter readiness. Target is maintenance triage, not passenger tracking; independent condition and staff-check nodes capture different observations around a stop.

Rejected in this run: apartment laundry availability (too close to WashWise); community-fridge stock monitoring (too close to PantryLink); public queue wait estimates (too close to QueueKind). Shelter readiness is distinct in user, environment and evidence model, though it shares the broad civic-infrastructure category with recent entries.
